About this calculator

A planning tool for Call of War. Two data sets power it:

  • Build costs — every unit (37 types across 4 doctrines — Allies, Axis, Comintern, Pan-Asian — plus base stats, levels 1–7) and building, with Food, Metal, Oil, Manpower and Money costs.
  • Nation economy — each nation's average daily resource production at game start, across 10 scenarios.

The original spreadsheets are included in the repo under /source.

How to use it

  1. Pick your doctrine, then add units and buildings with their level and quantity.
  2. Watch the total cost update live.
  3. Choose a scenario + nation under Days to afford to see how long your economy needs to pay for the build — the slowest resource is your bottleneck.
